    About and credits

    Hay River uses real data and a model that never saw the year shown. Fort Simpson and the Albany River show locked blind-test predictions (one per spring, outcomes not checked yet). All other sites are simulated examples. Not an official forecast.

    Contains RADARSAT Constellation Mission data © Government of Canada. RADARSAT is an official mark of the Canadian Space Agency. Gauge data: Water Survey of Canada (2025 and 2026 provisional). Climate: Environment and Climate Change Canada. Elevation: NRCan HRDEM. Basemap © Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
